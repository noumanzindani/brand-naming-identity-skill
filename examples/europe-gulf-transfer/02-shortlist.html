How to decide (the Lexicon way)

  • First ask "what could this name do for us?" — before anyone criticises. (Safir → an "ambassador" service tier; Akeed → every confirmation screen says the brand.)
  • Competitor test: "Careem Pay just launched a transfer product called ___ ." Which one makes you uncomfortable? That's a strong one.
  • A split reaction is a good sign. Intel's room split on Pentium. Unanimous comfort usually means tame.
  • If you test with users, test belief and pronunciation — never "which do you like?" Ask Arabic speakers in Europe: "Would you trust an app called ___ to send 1,000 € to your family?" and "Say it / spell it after hearing it once."

My recommendation

Safir leads on the scorecard (57) — premium, effortless in Arabic and European languages, and it carries all three associations. Akeed is the bold pick with the most emotional pull for a worried sender. If you feel a pull between those two, that split is useful: it's the choice between "dignified premium" and "warm certainty."
