2 · Onliness statement

Our app is the only money-transfer app that makes sending money from Europe to the Gulf feel as certain as handing it over in person, for Arabic-speaking families and workers in Europe, because every transfer is tracked, confirmed and explained in their own language — in an era of hidden fees and transfers that vanish for days.

Competitor-swap test: put "Wise" in — it claims cheap and fast, not certainty and Arabic-native care. Put "Western Union" in — it claims reach, not premium calm. The statement holds, so the differentiator is certainty + respect, not just speed or price.

3 · Three core associations

1. Certainty
It arrives. You'll know when. Nothing hidden.
2. Swiftness
Minutes, not days — effortless to send.
3. Dignified care
Premium, Arabic-native respect for the sender and the family receiving.
Everything the name and identity do must point to at least one of these — and never contradict "Certainty".

4 · Personality & voice

Traits: Assured · Calm · Warm-premium · Clear · Swift

Voice rule (X, not Y)Example

Direction kept: a short, premium name with an Arabic money/speed/trust root that also sounds clean in European languages.

6 · Hard gates (fail any → out)

  • Arabic-safe sound: no names that depend on "p" or "v" (Arabic has neither; they become b / f); pronounceable on first read in Arabic script and Latin.
  • Europe-safe meaning: no bad meaning in English, French, German, Spanish, Italian, Dutch.
  • Finance-safe: no interest/riba connotation (rate, yield, tenor), no gambling, no misuse of religious terms; avoid regulated words like "Bank" unless licensed as one.
  • Not confusable with an existing payment brand or central-bank system (e.g. Fawran, Aani, Jaywan, sarie, Benefit).
  • Store-ready: brand ≤ ~12 characters so "Brand: Send Money to the Gulf"-style titles fit 30 characters.
